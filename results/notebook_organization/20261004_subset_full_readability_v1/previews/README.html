Melanoma Detection

This project explores whether lesion images and their recorded age, sex and body location can help distinguish benign from malignant images. The notebooks show data preparation, model comparisons and checks of what those comparisons can establish. The work is exploratory; it does not establish a clinical screening tool.

Start with the notebook guide for a reading route and the status of each step. Subset contains preparation and modelling on the smaller dataset; Full contains preparation and exploration of the full collection. The short concept guide explains the metrics and validation terms used throughout.

For method details, read the metadata guide or image guide. Current image experiments use an inner validation split: patients separate from fitting, whose labels helped choose pilot settings. They are development diagnostics. Fusion and the reserved final test remain planned.

Image uncertainty reads existing checkpoints; its default is the weak EfficientNet-B0 control. New inference outputs go to separate directories under results/image_uncertainty_runs. Historical notebook guides explain the preserved original experiments. Renaming notebook folders leaves data, models, training histories and saved results unchanged; _d1/_d2 in artifact names remain their original identifiers.
