Reading the historical notebooks

These files preserve earlier experiments exactly, including their old titles, paths and saved outputs. Read them as records of how the project developed. Use the current notebook index for work with the current pipeline. The concept guide explains the shared terminology.

Original image model

image_model_three_epoch_fold0.ipynb contains the original three-epoch EfficientNet-B0 training recipe. A fold is a group of held-out patients; this prototype evaluated only fold 0. It has no saved cell outputs or figures. Its recorded metrics are cited inside the notebook from existing result files, so they do not represent a fresh execution of this copy.

Read the fold table as counts of images, patients and malignant images. Read AP and ROC-AUC as ranking measures, with higher values indicating better ranking. Lower Brier score and log loss indicate better agreement between scores and labels. Class-weighted training gives malignant images more influence; the resulting scores need calibration checks before they can describe risk.

For the current workflow, start with 09 Image B0 pilot. It reports checkpoints from the shared training pipeline, recorded recipes and a separate inner-validation patient split.

Original image uncertainty

image_uncertainty_three_epoch_fold0.ipynb contains the earlier 30-pass dropout and eight-view TTA analysis of 1,370 images from 84 held-out patients. Its results concern the old checkpoint and fold only. The word probability in historical column and axis names denotes a prediction score; the reliability check shows that those scores are poorly calibrated.

Read its four figures as follows:

  • MC convergence: the horizontal axis counts dropout passes; the vertical axis is the median absolute difference from the 30-pass mean or SD. Falling lines mean closer agreement with that reference. Zero at 30 is guaranteed by comparison with itself and does not prove convergence beyond 30 passes.
  • Four-panel uncertainty comparison: the upper histograms count images at each SD. The lower-left scatterplot compares dropout SD with TTA SD for each image. The lower-right panel compares mean absolute score error across groups ordered by SD. An upward line associates higher variation with larger error, but benign images dominate and their absolute error equals their score.
  • Reliability diagram: each point places a group's mean score on the horizontal axis and its observed malignant fraction on the vertical axis. Points below the diagonal indicate scores above the observed frequency. There are only 25 malignant images and no interval around those points.
  • Risk–coverage curve: start at 100% retained on the right and move left as more images are set aside. A lower line means lower mean score error among retained images at the same coverage. It does not measure reviewer outcomes.

The archive lacks score-ranking and random-referral baselines and patient-based intervals for key differences. Those comparisons are provided in 13 Image uncertainty for the current checkpoint pipeline.

Legacy preparation experiments

legacy_training.ipynb records early attempts to select a patient subset, assign folds and count missing metadata. It has no figures or model evaluation. In its fold table, count is the number of images, sum the number malignant and mean their fraction. Similar fractions suggest comparable class balance, rather than model quality. A missing-value count of zero after replacement means the rows now carry Unknown; it does not recover the original information. The old subset and paths differ from the current inputs.

Use Subset cleaning or Full cleaning, followed by the matching exploration notebook, for current data preparation.
